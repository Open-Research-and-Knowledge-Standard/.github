# Code of Conduct

## Participation standard

ORKS welcomes careful disagreement and expects every participant to help keep
project work safe, useful, and open to people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- critique an idea, change, or result without attacking its author;
- state uncertainty honestly and correct material errors;
- listen when someone describes harmful impact or asks for contact to stop;
- preserve consent, confidentiality, source attribution, and license terms;
- keep technical discussions relevant and leave room for others to take part.

The following conduct is not accepted:

- targeted insults, slurs, humiliation, or discriminatory treatment;
- threats, coercion, repeated unwanted contact, or deliberate intimidation;
- unwanted sexual or romantic remarks, images, or approaches;
- exposing or soliciting confidential, identifying, or contact information;
- impersonation, sabotage, or deliberate obstruction of project participation;
- retaliation for raising a concern honestly.

## Scope

This code applies to official ORKS repositories, issue and review threads, and
other channels explicitly operated for the project. Conduct elsewhere may be
considered only when it directly threatens a participant's safety, disrupts
an ORKS space, or falsely presents itself as an official project action.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abuse hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's report-content or report-abuse tools.
To request project enforcement, use the affected repository's Code of Conduct
report form to ask the maintainer for a private contact path. Omit names,
incident details, private messages, and other sensitive information from that
public request.

The maintainer will review reports as capacity permits, protect reporter
privacy where reasonably possible, and may remove content, close discussions,
limit participation, or ban accounts when necessary. No fixed response or
resolution time is promised.

Anyone handling a report must disclose a conflict of interest and avoid
participating in a decision where impartiality is not possible. Reports made
in bad faith may themselves violate this code.
